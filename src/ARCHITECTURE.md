# Architecture and experimental boundaries

## Data flow

1. `World` creates local numerical observations from resource, occupancy,
   internal/kinetic state, a writable environmental trace, and nearby continuous
   signals.
2. A seed-specific permutation and sign mask hide the physical meaning of every
   observation channel from agents.
3. `Agent` combines the observation, its recurrent state, and its previous
   output in a bounded recurrent workspace.
4. The agent emits a continuous vector. A second hidden permutation maps it to
   world coupling and communication channels.
5. On the next tick, the agent updates a predictive model from its error and a
   stochastic policy from experienced scalar value.
6. Energy-rich agents reproduce imperfect copies. No behavior is copied as a
   human-readable rule.

## Why these mechanisms exist

- Recurrent state permits temporally extended behavior without scripted memory.
- Prediction supplies a self-supervised learning target supplied by the world.
- A single persistence signal makes policy learning possible. With no value
  signal whatsoever, stochastic drift is the only optimization criterion.
- Mutation and inheritance enable open-ended population adaptation.
- Local continuous signals permit communication without a predefined language.
- A decaying writable scalar field permits persistent environmental modification
  without predefined artifact or tool categories.

## Controls

The ablation runner uses identical seeds and initial distributions while
changing one mechanism:

- `adaptive`: all mechanisms active;
- `frozen`: no within-lifetime weight updates;
- `memoryless`: recurrent state is reset before every decision;
- `signal_blocked`: signal outputs and their energy costs remain, but other
  agents receive zeros.
- `trace_blocked`: trace commands and their energy costs remain, but the field
  cannot be modified or observed.

The signal and trace controls are cost-matched. This isolates the causal pathway
from the energetic consequence of producing an anonymous output.

Differences support causal claims about those mechanisms only. They do not
support claims about phenomenal consciousness.

## Measures

`metrics.py` deliberately exposes separate descriptive measures instead of one
“sentience score.” A composite score would embed the designer's definition and
invite circular conclusions. Signal influence is calculated by intervening on
the received signal while holding the controller fixed; memory dependence uses
a zero-history policy counterfactual.

Confirmatory reports treat the seed as the independent unit, preserve paired
initialization across conditions, report uncertainty and paired randomization
tests, and correct the fixed contrast family for multiple testing. The runner
saves each seed-condition record immediately and will only resume it under the
same protocol hash.

## Remaining priors and limitations

The topology, bandwidth, recurrent update, learning rules, energy dynamics, and
reproduction remain designed. Randomizing interfaces reduces semantic leakage
but does not eliminate architectural priors. The simulator also cannot measure
private experience, and behavior alone may be generated by many mechanisms.

The population limit is a computational safety cap, not a biological carrying
capacity. Reports therefore expose the fraction of ticks at that cap and flag a
study when mean cap exposure exceeds the protocol's 5% validity threshold.
